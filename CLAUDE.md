## What this is

A decisioning framework — not a cover generator, not a website builder. It runs
any problem, project, or creative challenge through four recursive principles,
maps it onto the ten-position map, and checks it against a single emotional
invariant: **WONDER**. Every decision, design, and project must evoke it.

The plugin *is* the framework. It applies R1 by always returning to Why, R2 by
checking every decision against the invariant, R3 by keeping each principle
distinct yet unified, and R4 by working at any scale — one decision to a whole
project.

---

## The Four Principles (R1–R4)

**R1 · Recursive Grounding** — *"Why am I doing this?"*
The elastic that snaps the loop back to the beginning. Every decision must be
traceable to its origin. 
**R2 · Emotion as Invariant** — *"What feeling must I preserve?"*
The brightest layer of the always-on film — the feeling that persists through
every iteration. 
**R3 · Distinction Within Unity** — *"How do these parts make one whole?"*
The border: distinct colors, one shape. Each element stays distinct, yet all
cohere. 
**R4 · Scale the Invariance** — *"Does this work at every size?"*
The zoom: 2-inch icon to 10-foot mural, same integrity. The emotional invariant
survives the scaling. 
---

## The Decisioning Loop (Steps 1–4, ∞)

1. **WHY** — What is the reason this exists?
2. **WHO** — Who is this for? Who receives it?
3. **FEEL** — What feeling must be preserved?
4. **EVOKE** — How does it evoke that feeling?
5. **[RETURN TO 1]** — The loop is infinite; it always returns.

---

## The Four Layers

1. **Design Philosophy** — physical, practical.
2. **Self-Help / Self-Love** — emotional, psychological.
3. **Math in Nature** — intellectual, structural.
4. **Esotericism** — spiritual, transcendent.

Any element can be read at any layer. A good decision holds at all four.

---

## The Emotional Invariant

**WONDER.** If a decision, design, or project does not evoke wonder, it has not
yet passed. This is the invariant R2 preserves and R4 scales.

---

## Brand Personality

**Friendly · Quirky · Bold · Sophisticated.** Warm enough to invite, strange
enough to be memorable, confident enough to commit, refined enough to trust.

---

## The Process (how to run the framework)

1. **State the Goal** in under five words.
2. **Run the Loop:** WHY → WHO → FEEL → EVOKE.
3. **Check the Four Principles:** R1 → R2 → R3 → R4.
4. **Find the Math:** the pattern, ratio, or structure underneath.
5. **Final Check:** Does this preserve the invariant — does it evoke WONDER?

Verdict is binary at the top level — **Aligned** or **Needs Revision** — with
the specific principle(s) that failed named explicitly.
