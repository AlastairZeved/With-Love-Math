# The Icon Family

One family carries every visual cue on this page. No emoji appears anywhere in
the README or these docs; every mark is a hand-set SVG from the set below.

## Design rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Grid | 48 × 48 units, marks centered on the optical center |
| Stroke | 2.4 units, round caps and round joins |
| Style | Geometric line marks: line + arc + rect; at most one filled accent per mark |
| Color | Gold `#c9a227` on dark grounds, `#8a6f1f` on light grounds |
| Ribbon form | 64 × 64 near-black plate (`#0a0a0c`), 10-unit corner radius, hairline gold inner stroke (opacity 0.20), mark centered at 48-unit scale |

The Deep Surfaces ribbon treatment is the family's "in-page" form — a dark
surface with the gold mark laid over it, extending the banner's aesthetic into
the icon body. It is used in the chapter plates, the diagrams, and the WONDER
anchor.

## Family usage in the README

- Every chapter plate closes with its section icon in ribbon form at the right
  edge (`.github/assets/*-ribbon.svg`).
- The loop phases appear in the cycle diagram (`.github/assets/art-loop.svg`),
  each phase node carrying its phase mark.
- The four principles appear in the parallel callout cards
  (`.github/assets/art-pillars.svg`), each card headed by its principle mark.
- The verdicts appear in the verdict table and the install matrix as the
  supported/needs-revision marks (`.github/assets/icons/verdict-aligned.svg`,
  `verdict-revision.svg`).
- The WONDER anchor recurs at the framework's inner chamber
  (`.github/assets/art-wonder.svg`) and at the colophon plate.

All marks exist in plain form (transparent ground) and ribbon form (dark
plate); the gold stays constant between the two.
